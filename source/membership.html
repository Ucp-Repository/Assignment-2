LIBRARY GUIDELINES

Late Return Policy

Please return borrowed books on or before their due dates.

  • A fine of PKR 20 is charged per book for each overdue day.
  • Members are notified when their borrowing period is about to expire.
  • Members with outstanding fines must clear their dues before borrowing additional books.
  • Lost or damaged books must be replaced or paid for according to library policy.
  • Loan renewals are allowed once if another member has not reserved the book.
