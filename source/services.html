Our Library Services

We combine traditional library resources with modern technology to make learning easier, faster, and more accessible.

Explore Our Services

Everything you need for reading, studying, and research.

🔎

Online Catalogue

Search our library collection and find books by title, author, category, or subject.

How to Use

  1. Visit the library's information desk.
  2. Request access to the catalogue terminal.
  3. Search for your desired book.
  4. Note its availability and shelf location.

Available: During library opening hours.

📱

E-Books & Digital Reading

Enjoy digital reading resources and selected electronic books from the comfort of your home.

How to Access

  1. Become a registered library member.
  2. Request digital resource access.
  3. Log in using your membership details.
  4. Browse and read available e-books.

Available: 24 hours a day, subject to resource access.

🪑

Study Room Booking

Reserve a quiet study space for individual learning, group discussions, and research.

How to Book

  1. Visit the library reception desk.
  2. Provide your membership details.
  3. Select an available time slot.
  4. Confirm your booking with staff.

Timings: Monday – Saturday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM.

🖨️

Printing & Scanning

Print assignments, scan important documents, and make copies of permitted study materials.

How to Use

  1. Visit the printing counter.
  2. Submit your digital file or document.
  3. Choose printing or scanning options.
  4. Pay the applicable service charges.

Timings: Monday – Friday, 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM.

📶

Free Wi-Fi

Stay connected with complimentary internet access for research, studying, and educational activities.

How to Connect

  1. Visit the library premises.
  2. Connect to the library Wi-Fi network.
  3. Request the access password from reception.
  4. Start browsing and learning.

Available: During library opening hours.

Need Assistance?

Our library staff are available to help you with digital resources, bookings, and other services.
